# Bonzi sprites

`purple.png`: copied without modification from
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheRealKCFan20/BonziWORLD/0635b420251c07a94f8c90158ffe9e19198246ad/src/www/img/bonzi/purple.png

Retrieved 2026-09-29. Original Bonzi character/art attributed to Bonzi Software.
Sheet: 3400×3360 RGBA palette PNG, 599418 bytes; cells 200×160, 17 columns.
`icon.png` is the neutral frame cropped to its visible bounds and fitted in 32×32.
`animations.json` documents cell rectangles, sequences and the chosen 15 fps timing.
Indices were checked against the same revision's `src/www/js/bonziData.js`.
